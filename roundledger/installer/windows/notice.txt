RoundLedger - important information
====================================

What RoundLedger does
- Records the results of crash-game rounds you watch in Google Chrome on
  supported sites, and shows statistics about those past rounds.
- It is read-only. It never places bets, never clicks anything on the game
  page and never changes what the game sends or receives.

What RoundLedger does not do
- It does not predict future rounds. Crash-game results are generated by the
  operator at random, and each round is independent of the ones before it.
  Statistics describe the past only. No feature in this product is evidence
  that you can win more often.

Gambling responsibly
- Only gamble with money you can afford to lose. Set time and spending limits
  before you play. If gambling stops being fun, stop. Free, confidential help
  is available, for example at https://www.begambleaware.org/ (UK) or from
  your local gambling-support service.
- You must be of legal gambling age in your jurisdiction, and gambling must be
  legal where you are. Using third-party tools may breach a gambling site's
  terms of service; check before you use RoundLedger.

Installation method
- Chrome on Windows only allows extensions from the Chrome Web Store to be
  installed automatically. RoundLedger is distributed directly, so this
  installer copies the extension to your computer and then shows you how to
  load it with Chrome's "Load unpacked" option, which needs Developer mode.
  Chrome may show a reminder about developer-mode extensions; this is expected.
